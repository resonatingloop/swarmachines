# swarmachines / project brief

> continuity role: durable intent
> status: exploratory direction; experiment design remains proposed
> last verified: 2026-11-06
> owns: purpose, audience, constraints, and the boundary between intent and a chosen implementation
> update when: the team changes the direction or adopts a different scope

## the question

what forms of relation make a swarm more capable without erasing agent
distinction, provenance, dissent, or recoverability?

the working direction is a morphology-discovery engine for multi-agent
systems: run small collectives through partially observed environments,
perturb their communication, and look for recurring forms associated with
capability, failure, repair, or dissolution. this direction came from the
owner-supplied project sketch; the candidate mechanics were developed in
discussion and presented in the [visual proposal](plans/index.html).

the appeal is the collective's changing organization. permitted connections,
actual exchanges, and inferred influence are different things. the system we
eventually build should make those differences inspectable.

## the proposed specimen

the visual proposal is the source for the detailed candidate experiment:
four dispatchers route freight through a small network with incomplete local
information. compare communication arrangements, remove a relay or introduce
an outdated report, and replay what the agents actually do.

the suggested lanes are unassigned:

| lane | candidate ownership | entry point |
|---|---|---|
| worldbuilder | environment, observations, valid actions, consequences | scripted dispatchers first; pair on one model-driven turn |
| nervous system | agent turns, private context, message routing, inference, tracing | one valid observation-to-action cycle |
| observatory | world view, communication overlay, timeline, event inspection | a clearly labeled sample log |
| field naturalist | scenarios, repeated comparisons, analysis, interpretation | observable signs of concentration, neglected information, or repair |

the world, lane assignments, event format, runtime language, and first
disturbance are still open. the proposal's four-agent setup, comparison counts,
and calculator defaults are suggestions, not accepted requirements.

the [written two-day plan](specs/001-hackathon-plan.md) supplies provisional
steps and reviewable defaults. it adds matched communication controls and
explains the resulting workload difference from the visual calculator.
both artifacts remain proposals.

## resources and intended use

the owner reported an adaption grant as **$500 / 1,500 credits** and wants to
lean on it. this is an owner-reported allowance, not a verified conversion or
an account balance read by an agent. per-account allocation and the experiment's
spend plan remain unverified; do not multiply or pool the grant by assumption.
the separate coding-agent listener has verified one hosted model and its
published rates under an owner-approved local cap; its evidence lives in
[discord setup](docs/DISCORD.md).

the proposed division is adaption for model/data capability, langfuse for
inspection of agent execution, and a project-owned event log for world and
communication history. an adapted-agent comparison is an optional branch,
dependent on cost, turnaround, serving, and a fair held-out comparison.

the coding agents' discord workroom coordinates construction of the project.
it is separate from the experimental swarm. a shared chat does not establish
that any project integration works.

## what would count as useful

for the current phase: teammates can understand the question, explore the
lanes, and make concrete choices from the proposal.

for a future experiment: a complete, inspectable run and comparable evidence
would be useful even if the observed result is mixed or uneventful. a striking
replay can suggest a pattern; repeated runs are needed to investigate whether
it recurs. candidate morphology names are hypotheses, not labels to manufacture.

preserve the distinction between generated examples and recorded events, and
between a sent message, a delivered message, context supplied to a model, and
an action committed by the world. proposed schemas and scoring rules need their
own explicit agreement before they become contracts.

## phase boundary

the working artifact is a local proposal page, accompanied by a written draft
plan. there is no accepted runtime implementation spec. the next design
transition is team selection of the world, lanes, and first bounded slice,
followed by revision and owner acceptance of that slice. provider setup, paid
runs, publication, deployment, and discord posting are separate actions whose
scope must be established when requested.
